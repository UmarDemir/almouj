    How the model works

    Sample

    50 public listings

    Wave Homes and Dubizzle, September 2025

    Asking prices ranged 90,000–300,000 OMR (mean 165,410 OMR), with a right-skewed distribution corrected by a log transform before fitting.

    Mean price per m²: 1,175.8 OMR.

    Model

    F = 35.29

    Multiple linear regression, p < 0.001

    Strongest correlations with log price: marina view (r = 0.83), bedrooms (r = 0.80) and surface area (r = 0.79).

    Parking spaces were dropped to reduce multicollinearity. Surface area and bedrooms had VIF above 100 before feature selection.

    Validation

    MAE 10,902 OMR

    On 10 new listings from OpenSooq

    50 listings: 40 for training, 10 held out (R² 0.90). Then tested on 10 new listings from OpenSooq, unseen during training.

    MLR (MAE 10,902 OMR, RMSE 14,445 OMR) generalized better than a principal component regression alternative (MAE 15,831 OMR), so the calculator uses MLR.

    This tool estimates asking-price levels from a small sample (n = 50) of one community and is not a formal appraisal. Limitations include source bias toward asking rather than sale prices, and reduced accuracy at the extremes of the size range.

    Methodology reviewed by Prof. Abraham Varghese, PhD (University of Technology and Applied Sciences, Muscat).
