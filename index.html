What's driving this number
Prediction = exp(11.2307 + 0.0012·sqm + 0.2110·bed + 0.0320·bath − 0.0377·balcony + 0.0125·floor + location + view + furnishing). Coefficients fit by OLS on log-transformed price, n = 40 training rows, tested on 10 held-out listings.
Sample

50 verified listings

Sourced from Wave Homes and Dubizzle in September 2025. Prices ranged 90,000–300,000 OMR with a right-skewed distribution, corrected with a log transform before fitting.

Mean price/m²: 1,175.8 OMR
Model

Multiple linear regression

Parking spaces were dropped for severe multicollinearity (VIF > 100). Marina view and surface area were the strongest correlates of price (r = 0.83 and r = 0.79).

F-statistic 35.29, p < 0.001
Validation

Tested out-of-sample

A separate 10-apartment set, unseen during training, was used to test real-world accuracy. MLR generalized better here than a principal component regression alternative.

Mean absolute error: 10,902 OMR

△ This tool estimates asking-price levels from a small (n=50) sample of one community and should not be treated as a formal appraisal — see the accompanying IA for full limitations, including source bias toward asking (not sale) prices and reduced accuracy at the extremes of the size range. Built with Python (pandas, statsmodels, scikit-learn) and deployed as a static client-side calculator.
